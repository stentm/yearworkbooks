Quick Reading & General Knowledge

Reading Comprehension

Jamie and her little brother Sam went to the seaside with their grandad on a sunny Saturday. They packed a picnic with sandwiches, apples, and a big bottle of lemonade. When they arrived, Sam ran straight towards the waves, laughing loudly, while Jamie stayed on the sand to build a tall sandcastle with a deep moat around it. After lunch, grandad took them rockpooling and they found a small crab and two shiny shells. By the time they left, everyone was tired, sandy, and very happy.

Passage 2: Hedgehogs (non-fiction)

Hedgehogs are small, spiky animals that live in gardens and woodlands across Britain. They are nocturnal, which means they sleep during the day and come out at night to search for food. A hedgehog's favourite foods are slugs, beetles, and worms. When a hedgehog feels frightened, it curls up into a tight ball so that only its sharp spines are showing, which keeps it safe from predators like foxes. In winter, hedgehogs hibernate — they find a cosy pile of leaves and sleep until spring arrives.

Passage 3: The Treasure Map (fiction)

On Saturday morning, twins Mia and Leo found an old, faded map hidden inside a dusty book in their grandmother's attic. The map showed a path through the back garden, past the oak tree, and down to a spot marked with a red X. Excited, they grabbed a spade and followed the path exactly as the map showed. After digging carefully for ten minutes, Leo's spade hit something hard. They pulled out a small wooden box, and inside it was a handful of old coins and a note that read: "Well done, treasure hunters!" It turned out their grandmother had buried it there years ago as a game for whoever found the map.

Passage 4: Sports Day (fiction)

On Friday, Oakwood Primary held its yearly Sports Day on the school field. Ben felt nervous before the sack race, but his best friend Priya cheered loudly from the side. When the whistle blew, Ben hopped as fast as he could and crossed the finish line in second place, winning a shiny silver ribbon. Later, the whole class cheered for Priya as she won the running race, finishing first by just one second. At the end of the day, Mrs Clarke gave out medals and reminded everyone that trying your best was the most important thing of all.

      🔒 Privacy — in plain English

      This app does not create an account for your child, does not track them, and does not send anything to us automatically.

      • Everything your child types (answers, score, their first name) is saved only on this device, in the browser's local storage — never sent anywhere.
      • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies.
      • The only time anything leaves this device is if you choose to press "📧 Send by email" above — that just opens your own email app with the message ready to send.
      • The app loads its font from a file bundled with it, not from an external service — so nothing is requested from anywhere else on the internet while it's open.
      • You can erase all saved progress at any time with the "Start over" link at the bottom of the screen.

      This is a plain-language summary, not a formal legal policy — happy to expand it if you need something more formal.
