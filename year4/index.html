Doubling the Final Consonant

When a short word ends in one vowel + one consonant, double the last letter before adding an ending like -ing or -ed. Example: run → running.

Tricky 'ch' Words (sounds like "sh")

Reading Comprehension

The Great Fire of London began in the early hours of 2 September 1666, in a bakery on Pudding Lane. A strong wind quickly spread the flames through the narrow, crowded streets, where most houses were built from wood. The fire burned for four days and destroyed thousands of homes, along with old St Paul's Cathedral. Remarkably, only a handful of people are recorded to have died. Afterwards, the city was rebuilt with wider streets and many more buildings made from brick and stone, to help prevent such a disaster from happening again.

📗 Passage 2: The Amazon Rainforest

The Amazon Rainforest is the largest rainforest in the world, covering large parts of South America. It is home to millions of different plants and animals, including jaguars, toucans and poison dart frogs. Scientists believe it contains more types of trees than anywhere else on Earth. The rainforest produces a huge amount of the world's oxygen, which is why people sometimes call it the "lungs of the Earth". Sadly, large areas of rainforest are cut down every year for farming and building, which puts many animals' homes at risk.

Fiction Story: The Secret Room

Mia stood outside the old library, staring at the enormous wooden door. According to her grandmother, a secret room lay hidden somewhere behind the shelves, but nobody had found the entrance in over fifty years. Mia's hands trembled as she pulled a rusty, old-fashioned key from her pocket — the very key her grandmother had given her that morning, wrapped in a faded velvet cloth. She slid it into the lock. For a moment, nothing happened. Then, with a groan like an old ship's timber, the door creaked open just wide enough for her to slip inside. Dust danced in the thin beam of light from her torch, and somewhere deep within the shadows, she thought she heard the soft, unmistakable sound of turning pages.

      🔒 Privacy — in plain English

      This app does not create an account for your child, does not track them, and does not send anything to us automatically.

      • Everything your child types (answers, score, their first name) is saved only on this device, in the browser's local storage — never sent anywhere.
      • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies.
      • The only time anything leaves this device is if you choose to press "📧 Send by email" above — that just opens your own email app with the message ready to send.
      • The app loads its font from a file bundled with it, not from an external service — so nothing is requested from anywhere else on the internet while it's open.
      • You can erase all saved progress at any time with the "Start over" link at the bottom of the screen.

      This is a plain-language summary, not a formal legal policy — happy to expand it if you need something more formal.
